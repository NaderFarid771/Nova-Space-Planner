SMALL DETAILS, BIG DIFFERENCE

Make every corner count.

Three simple principles to make a compact workspace feel open and personal.

01 / LIGHT

Let daylight lead

Place your desk near a window when possible and keep the brightest area clear.

02 / ORDER

Give things a home

Use vertical storage and a small tray to keep essentials close but clutter out of sight.

03 / COLOR

Keep a calm palette

Pair one grounding shade with warm neutrals, then add one accent you love.
